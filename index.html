FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else, just ask. You'll get me, not a bot.

Do I need to learn any AI tools?

Nope. I set everything up and run it for you. If you want something changed, you text me and it's done. That's the whole point.

What if the assistant gets something wrong?

It only answers from what it's been taught about your business, and anything important like bookings, quotes or site changes waits for your approval. I also read through the chats and keep tuning it.

Can I keep my domain and current website?

Yes. I'll connect your existing domain, and if you like parts of your current site we can bring them over. Your free preview shows you exactly what you'd get first.

What happens if I cancel?

No contract, so you can cancel anytime. Your domain stays yours, and I'll help you move things over if you need to. No hard feelings :)
